Back Road Hog: third-party notices
==================================

Back Road Hog is a personal, non-commercial route planner. Its installer bundles the
open-source components listed below, each under its own licence. None of them were modified.
Source code for each is available from the listed project or registry (crates.io, npmjs.com,
Maven Central).

DATA
- Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- Basemap tiles: Protomaps (https://protomaps.com), built from OpenStreetMap (ODbL).
- Texas outline (texas.geojson): derived from Geofabrik's texas.poly, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- TxDOT speed-limit and road-surface data is NOT included. Each installation downloads it directly
  from the Texas Department of Transportation for personal, non-commercial use, under TxDOT's terms.
